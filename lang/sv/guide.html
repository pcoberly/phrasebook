Swedish

Swedish is spoken by about 10 million people in Sweden and by the Swedish-speaking minority in Finland, where it is an official language. Norwegians and Danes read it easily. Nearly every Swede speaks excellent English, so these phrases are about the smile. The "sounds like" spellings put the stressed syllable in capitals. Swedish has a sing-song melody: copy the audio.

Being polite

Swedes say du, you, to everyone, and use first names. There is no everyday please: put tack, thanks, at the end of a request instead. Queues matter, often with a ticket number. And fika, a coffee break with something sweet, is a national institution: join in if you're invited.
