Romanian

Romanian is spoken by about 24 million people in Romania and Moldova. It is a Latin language, a cousin of Italian, French and Spanish, with words borrowed from its Slavic neighbours. If you know a Romance language, much of it will look familiar. The "sounds like" spellings put the stressed syllable in capitals.

Being polite

These phrases use the polite dumneavoastră forms (verbs ending in -ți), right for strangers. Add vă rog, please, to requests. Romanians are warm hosts: expect offers of food and a glass of țuică, plum brandy, in the countryside. Noroc! is both cheers and bless you.
