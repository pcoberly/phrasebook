Greek

Greek is spoken by about 13 million people in Greece and Cyprus, and its alphabet is the ancestor of the Latin one, so many letters look familiar. Stress matters and is always marked with an accent (ά, έ, ή...), shown in capitals in the "sounds like" spellings. The question mark is written like a semicolon: Τι κάνετε;

Yes and no

Ναι (ne) means yes, which catches everyone out. A small tilt of the head back, often with a click of the tongue, means no.

Being polite

These phrases use the polite plural "you", right for strangers and older people. Greetings change through the day: καλημέρα in the morning, καλησπέρα from the afternoon. Hospitality, filoxenía, is a point of pride: accept a coffee or a treat when it's offered.
