A note on accuracy

These Khmer phrases are a first draft. Have a Khmer speaker check them before you rely on them, and trust the audio over the "sounds like" spellings, which are only rough.

The script

Khmer is written with its own script, an abugida in which each consonant carries a vowel, and extra marks change or replace it. There are no spaces between words, only between phrases. Romanization has no single standard, so the Latin spellings on signs and menus vary.

No tones

Unlike Vietnamese or Lao, Khmer has no tones. The pitch of your voice does not change a word's meaning. The stress usually falls on the last syllable.

Numbers and money

Counting goes from 1 to 5 with its own words, then 6 to 9 are "five plus one" to "five plus four". Prices are in Cambodian riel or US dollars, and often both in the same shop. Small change is given in riel, at about 4,000 riel to the dollar.
