A note on accuracy

These Lao phrases are a first draft. Have a Lao speaker check them before you rely on them, and trust the audio over the "sounds like" spellings, which are only rough. Lao tones are not marked in the app, so copying the audio matters more here than in the other languages.

The script

Lao has its own script, written left to right, in which each consonant carries a vowel and extra marks change it. It looks like Thai but is simpler. There are no spaces between words, only between phrases. Romanization has no single standard, so spellings on signs and menus vary.

Tones

Lao is a tonal language, with six tones in Vientiane. The tone is part of the word, so the same syllable can mean different things. The tone depends on the consonant and the vowel, which is why the app does not label each syllable. Listen to the audio and copy the melody of the whole phrase.

Numbers and money

Prices are in Lao kip, which is worth very little, so numbers get large: ພັນ (pan) is a thousand, ແສນ (saen) a hundred thousand and ລ້ານ (lan) a million. Thai baht and US dollars are also taken in many places.
