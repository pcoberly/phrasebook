Getting started

Choose the language you speak and the one you want to learn. PhraseBook downloads it once, about 2 MB, and from then on everything works without a connection.

To open PhraseBook like any other app, add it to your Home Screen: More → Install on this device shows how on your phone.

The tabs at the bottom take you to the List, Cards, Quiz, Guide and More.

The List

Every phrase, grouped by topic: what it means in your language, the phrase itself, how to say it, and a note on when to use it.

  • 🔊 plays the phrase. Play it to the person you're talking to, or hand them the phone.
  • The sounds-like spelling is written for your language. Capitals mark the stressed syllable; accents mark tones in Vietnamese, Thai and Chinese.
  • A woman says appears where a woman says a phrase differently, and the female voice says that form.
  • In a phrase with a gap, like "Which train for…?", the highlighted word is the example the audio uses. Put in your own.
  • The topic button shows just the topics you choose, and the direction box decides which language comes first.
  • 🔊 Sound on turns the automatic speech off, on a bus say. 🔊 and Play all still play when you tap them.

Search

Tap 🔍 on the List and type a word in your language or in the one you're learning. It looks in every topic, accents and capitals don't matter, and matches are highlighted. ✕ closes the search.

Play all

▶ Play all on the List reads the phrases aloud: your language, a pause, then the phrase. It's made for walks and journeys, and keeps going with the screen locked. It plays what the List shows, so choose topics or search first to hear just those. In Settings you can change the pause and have each phrase said twice.

Cards

Flashcards, one phrase at a time. Tap the card to flip it, then say how you did: ✓ Knew it or ✗ Didn't know it. A card you know comes back after longer and longer gaps (1, 3, 7, 14, then 30 days), and one you missed comes back soon. Each phrase is two cards, one in each direction.

Listening only hides the written phrase, so you practise understanding what you hear.

Quiz

Rounds of multiple-choice questions: pick the right answer out of four. At the end you see your score and the phrases to review. Answers count towards your progress, just like the cards. Choose how long a round is in Settings.

Practise or leave out

Each phrase has a ✓ circle. Tap it to empty it, and the phrase is left out of the cards, the quiz and Play all, such as the beer phrase if you don't drink. It stays in the List, faded. Tap the circle again to bring it back, or bring them all back in Settings → Left out of practice.

Guide

How the language works: its alphabet or script, the sounds that surprise, tones, politeness, and numbers and money. Worth reading once before a trip.

Languages and progress

More → Languages is where you switch between your languages, Add a language, or remove one with ✕ to free its space. It also shows how many cards you've learned in each. Your progress is kept for each language, even after you remove it.

Some languages are marked draft: a native speaker hasn't checked them yet. Trust the audio over the spelling, and check anything important with a local.

Offline and updates

Once a language is downloaded, everything works without a connection: the phrases, the audio, the cards, the quiz, the guide and this help. When you're online, PhraseBook keeps itself up to date; if a new version arrives while you're using it, the bottom of the screen says so, and a tap reloads. More → What's new lists the changes.

Your privacy

There's no account. Your progress and settings stay on your phone. The app counts anonymous visits and which features are used, with no cookies and no personal data. To tell us about a wrong phrase or an idea, use More → Send feedback.
